Our story

Gain from our experience.

American Buying Service traces its roots to 1994, when JSJ Enterprise Co., Ltd. and Point Image (Thailand) Co., Ltd. were established in Bangkok, Thailand. In 1998, American Buying Service joined the organization as an affiliate, extending its operations to Los Angeles, California. The businesses were later brought together under Point Image Inc., a holding organization that eventually became Mstar Inc.

American Buying Service began as a specialized buying and procurement office, providing sourcing support to leading international brands and retailers, including Nordstrom, Macy’s, Hudson’s Bay, Polo Ralph Lauren, K&R Sportswear, Cradle Tog, Baby Tog, Warner Bros., Little Me, Carter’s, and Disney. Today, more than 200 professionals worldwide support clients across global markets. In 2015, American Buying Service expanded its capabilities to include online marketing, product formulation and packaging, fulfillment, and logistics—helping international online retailers enter the U.S. market, reduce freight costs, and manage their American operations more efficiently.

As commerce continues to shift from traditional retail stores to online channels, American Buying Service has developed practical strategies that help manufacturers compete more effectively in the U.S. market. By reducing shipping expenses, improving pricing competitiveness, and strengthening fulfillment and customer service, American Buying Service enables overseas manufacturers to reach consumers directly and reduce their reliance on traditional intermediaries.

American Buying Service gives international businesses the experience, infrastructure, and support needed to establish a strong presence in the United States. For organizations ready to enter new markets, strengthen their operations, and build greater global recognition, American Buying Service is a trusted partner for long-term growth.

Our Global Support

How Can We Help Your Company Succeed?

American Buying Service helps international companies establish a professional presence in the United States, strengthen their credibility, and deliver dependable service to their customers. Our experienced team works as an extension of each client’s business, providing the operational support, market knowledge, and customer care needed to succeed in the U.S. market.

Our services include:

  • Providing international companies with a private U.S. business address and dedicated staff trained to understand their products.
  • Assigning trained customer service representatives who can provide detailed product information and assist customers on the client’s behalf.
  • Helping clients strengthen their credibility and reputation as registered U.S. businesses.
  • Repackaging and barcoding products according to each client’s requirements.
  • Processing returned merchandise by restocking it or arranging shipment back to the client.
  • Issuing approved refunds to a client’s customers for returned merchandise.
  • Providing 24/7 call-center support to answer questions and address customer concerns on the client’s behalf.
  • Preparing customs-clearance documentation and coordinating the pickup and transportation of containers to our warehouse.
  • Arranging truck deliveries to retail stores and distribution centers.
  • Representing clients and promoting their products at trade shows.
  • Supporting clients with search engine optimization, digital marketing, and social media marketing.
  • Assisting with business negotiations and representing the client’s interests.
  • Helping startups bring their ideas to market through product formulation, packaging design, distribution, sales, and marketing support.
  • Supporting startups at every stage—from product formulation and packaging design to distribution, sales, and marketing.

At American Buying Service, our customers are at the center of everything we do. We continually improve our service, streamline operations, and identify opportunities to reduce costs because our success is directly connected to the success of our customers. By building trusted, long-term relationships, we gain a deeper understanding of each customer’s needs and provide dependable solutions that support lasting growth.

Our fulfillment expertise, advanced technology, and commitment to responsive service enable us to deliver efficient solutions in an increasingly competitive marketplace. We continuously evaluate and improve our processes to help customers lower operating costs, strengthen their businesses, and remain competitive within their industries. Our goal is simple: to help every customer succeed—because when our customers grow, American Buying Service grows with them.
