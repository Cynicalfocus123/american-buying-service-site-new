About American Buying Service

Built to help global brands grow in the U.S.

Since 1994, we have brought buying, sourcing, fulfillment, logistics, and customer service together for businesses entering the American market.

Our story

Gain from our experience.

The company was established in 1994 under JSJ Enterprise Co., Ltd. and Point Image (Thailand) Co., Ltd. in Bangkok, Thailand. In 1998, the company added American Buying Service (ABS) as a new affiliate and expanded its office to Los Angeles, California. Later, the company incorporated all businesses into a holding company under Point Image Inc., which later became Mstar Inc.

The company began as a buying office and procurement service, offering buying and sourcing services to Nordstrom, Macy's, Hudson's Bay, Polo Ralph Lauren, K & R Sportswear, Cradle Tog, Baby Tog, Warner Bros., Little Me, Carter, Disney, and more. American Buying Service employs more than 200 people worldwide to serve customers globally. In 2015, the company expanded into online marketing, formulation and packaging, fulfillment-center, and logistics services to help overseas online retailers reduce freight costs and complete their online business in the U.S.

The company recognizes the shift from brick-and-mortar stores to rapidly growing online business. We have developed strategies that help factories save on shipping costs, compete with U.S. importers on price, and deliver stronger service. Overseas factories can sell directly to consumers and bypass middlemen.

American Buying Service helps companies expand in the U.S. and compete with confidence. If your company is ready to grow and you want your brand known around the world, we are here for you.
