# American Buying Service Design Rules

These rules preserve the approved site direction without locking future content
into a rigid template.

## Brand Direction

- Primary colors are deep navy, American Buying Service orange, white, and restrained
  neutral grays.
- Use orange for active states, links, rules, and important calls to action. Avoid
  large orange backgrounds unless the user explicitly requests one.
- Maintain strong contrast and a professional international-logistics appearance.
- Use only approved logo files inside this project. Preserve transparency, aspect
  ratio, and complete logo artwork; never stretch, recolor, or crop off brand text.
- The current preferred transparent logo is `American Buying Service Logo 2.png`.

## Header

- The homepage header overlays only the top hero section.
- Keep the header dark navy, translucent, and moderately blurred so the video remains
  visible without reducing navigation readability.
- Keep the logo clearly visible at the far left.
- Place navigation or the menu trigger beside the logo and utility controls at the
  far right.
- On desktop, show Home, About Us, Our Services, Products, and Contact Us as visible
  navigation links. Show the hamburger menu only on tablet and mobile.
- Do not add an opaque white header background unless the user requests it.
- Inner pages may use a stronger translucent background when no hero media sits
  behind the header.

## Hero

- Use only `business-american-buying-service.mp4` for the current homepage hero.
- The hero contains one stable HTML5 video element—no video carousel and no alternate
  source from another folder.
- Preserve `autoplay muted loop playsinline`; use a production poster when one exists.
- Keep overlay text short and readable. Do not obscure the main video subject.
- Keep the homepage video hero free of tracking or utility panels unless the user
  explicitly requests a new one.

## Solutions Section

- Keep the “Our Solutions” section centered and spacious on desktop.
- Use nine image-backed service panels with consistent borders, unique line icons,
  headings, supporting text, and hover/focus behavior.
- Panels may become two columns on tablet and one column on mobile.
- Mobile must expose essential descriptions without requiring hover.
- Use project-local imagery only. Do not reproduce third-party logos or branding.

## Footer

- Keep the approved logo visible in the footer.
- Maintain clear contact information, restrained typography, and strong contrast.
- Do not introduce unrelated navigation, social accounts, or business claims without
  verified content from the user.

## Responsive Rules

- Verify at minimum 1440px desktop, 768px tablet, and 390px mobile after layout work.
- Also check 320px when changing the header, menu, tracking panel, or long text.
- No page-level horizontal overflow is allowed.
- Do not solve mobile problems by hiding essential content.
- Touch targets should be approximately 44px and keyboard focus must remain visible.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Copy and Accessibility

- Grammar-check visible text before saving.
- Use one clear page-level heading and logical heading order.
- Provide meaningful alternative text for content images and empty alternative text
  for purely decorative images.
- Preserve form labels, accessible button names, keyboard operation, and sufficient
  color contrast.
- Do not claim shipping times, savings, locations, or capabilities unless the wording
  is approved or already present in verified project content.

## Change Discipline

- Change only the requested section unless a dependent responsive or accessibility
  fix is required.
- Preserve approved body sections when a request is limited to the header or hero.
- Summarize material visual changes and the breakpoints verified.
- Replace obsolete rules in this file instead of adding a changelog.
